**AI Application:**
- 24/7 booking agent handling intake, qualification, payment collection
- Integration with FareHarbor/booking platform
- Smart routing to human team (complex requests)
- Automated confirmations, reminders, follow-ups
- Peak-season overflow handling

**Outreach Angle:**
"Reclaim 15+ hours/week from booking questions + close bookings your team misses overnight."

### 2.2 Database Fields Explained

| `created_at` | ISO8601 | Date prospect entered database |
| `updated_at` | ISO8601 | Last modification timestamp |

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| `name` | string | Decision maker full name |

| Field | Type | Purpose |

### 2.3 Integration Methods

**For Hermes Agent Integration:**

```bash
# Load prospects from database

# Update prospect status

# Log outreach activity
hermes log-activity [prospect_id] --type email_sent --template message_1

# Fetch prospects by filter
```

## PART 5: DEMO → IMPLEMENTATION WORKFLOW

### 5.1 Sales Pipeline Stages

```
Lead → Qualified → Assessment Scheduled → Assessment Completed → 
Demo Scheduled → Demo Conducted → Proposal → Negotiation → 
Signed → Implementation → Deployed → Success
```

### 5.2 Stage Definitions & Actions

#### Stage 1: Lead
- **Definition:** Prospect in database, initial outreach sent, no response yet
- **Duration:** 7-21 days (across 3-email follow-up sequence)
- **Action Items:**
  - Send 3 follow-up emails (7, 14, 21 days)
  - Track opens, clicks, responses
  - Research prospect deeper (website, competitors, recent news)
  - Identify additional decision makers if possible
- **Exit Criteria:** Response received OR moved to "disqualified" after 21 days

#### Stage 2: Qualified
- **Definition:** Prospect responded, expressed interest or asked question
- **Duration:** 3-7 days
- **Action Items:**
  - Send detailed response (personalized to their specific question)
  - Provide 2-3 assessment time slots (next 7 days)
  - Share one relevant case study or guide
  - Clarify engagement model (custom build, 2-week timeline, ongoing)
- **Exit Criteria:** Assessment scheduled OR prospect disqualifies ("too expensive," "not interested")

#### Stage 3: Assessment Scheduled
- **Definition:** Prospect has booked 15-minute discovery call
- **Duration:** 1-7 days (before call)
- **Action Items:**
  - Send calendar confirmation + video link (Zoom or phone)
  - Prepare: Prospect research packet, 2-3 relevant questions, case study
  - Template: Pre-call email with "what to expect"
  - Reminder email 24 hours before
- **Exit Criteria:** Assessment call conducted

#### Stage 4: Assessment Completed
- **Definition:** 15-minute discovery call completed
- **Duration:** Same day - 3 days after
- **Action Items:**
  - Document findings (pain points, tools, decision timeline, budget hints)
  - Identify: Specific AI opportunity, estimated WTP, implementation timeline
  - Create: Custom one-page opportunity analysis (personalized to them)
  - Send: Follow-up email with analysis + next step (demo, proposal, or decision timeline)
- **Decision at this stage:**
  - **Move to Demo:** If prospect genuinely interested + fit is strong
  - **Move to "No Fit":** If mismatch too large
  - **Move to "Future":** If interested but timing/budget not right

#### Stage 5: Demo Scheduled
- **Definition:** Prospect agreed to see working prototype/demo
- **Duration:** 3-14 days (build time + demo date)
- **Action Items:**
  - Build: Lightweight custom demo (or heavily tailored existing system)
  - Show: Specific to their workflows, connected to their tools if possible
  - Prepare: 20-min demo, 10-min Q&A
  - Send: Demo briefing (what they'll see, what it does, what it doesn't do yet)
  - Pre-demo prep call (optional, clarify expectations)
- **Exit Criteria:** Demo presented

#### Stage 6: Demo Conducted
- **Definition:** Live demo or prototype presented to prospect
- **Duration:** Same day - 3 days after
- **Action Items:**
  - Record: Feedback, objections, excitement level
  - Document: Next steps they want to see
  - Send: Thank-you email + proposal (if ready) or roadmap for next phase
  - Gauge: Is this moving toward "let's do it" or "we need to think"?
- **Decision at this stage:**
  - **Move to Proposal:** If enthusiasm is clear
  - **Move to Negotiation:** If "yes but price/terms"
  - **Move to "Thinking":** If they need internal discussion
  - **Move to "No":** If it doesn't resonate

#### Stage 7: Proposal Sent
- **Definition:** Formal written proposal delivered
- **Duration:** 1-7 days (creation + sending)
- **Action Items:**
  - Proposal includes: Scope, timeline (2-3 week build), pricing, terms, what's included
  - Cover: Ongoing support (monthly check-ins, iteration budget)
  - Price point: Based on estimated WTP + complexity
  - Include: Case study example, testimonial, risk mitigation (custom build, embedded approach)
  - Send: With context email (not just attachment)
- **Exit Criteria:** Prospect reviews proposal, responds with yes/no/revise

#### Stage 8: Negotiation
- **Definition:** Back-and-forth on pricing, timeline, scope, or terms
- **Duration:** 3-14 days
- **Action Items:**
  - Document: All counter-proposals and responses
  - Flexibility: Adjust scope/timeline/price within reason
  - Principle: Stay firm on embedded approach (can't commoditize)
  - Find: Mutual win (they get value, you get engagement)
  - Escalate: If needed (higher price justified by better outcomes, shorter timeline)
- **Exit Criteria:** Signed agreement OR "too high" rejection

#### Stage 9: Signed
- **Definition:** Agreement signed, contract executed, project kick-off scheduled
- **Duration:** 1-7 days before kick-off
- **Action Items:**
  - Send: Kick-off email with project timeline + first meeting
  - Collect: Access credentials (booking platform, CRM, email, etc.)
  - Prepare: Intake document (workflow deep-dive, system requirements)
  - Schedule: Week 1 meeting (2 hours, full team present)
- **Exit Criteria:** Kick-off meeting held

#### Stage 10: Implementation
- **Definition:** Active build phase (2-3 weeks)
- **Duration:** 14-21 days
- **Action Items:**
  - Daily/weekly async updates (Slack, email)
  - Demonstration of components as they're built
  - Feedback loops (prospect reviews, requests changes)
  - Integration testing (connection to their tools)
  - User training prep (documentation, video tutorials)
- **Exit Criteria:** System ready for deployment

#### Stage 11: Deployed
- **Definition:** System live, team trained, monitoring active
- **Duration:** 1-3 days (go-live week)
- **Action Items:**
  - Monitor: System performance, error rates, user adoption
  - Support: Hand-on support during first week
  - Team training: Hands-on + documentation
  - First month: Daily check-ins (identify issues quickly)
- **Exit Criteria:** System stable, team comfortable, metrics established

#### Stage 12: Success
- **Definition:** System live for 30+ days, delivering results
- **Duration:** Ongoing (monthly check-ins)
- **Action Items:**
  - Monthly review: Metrics, improvements, feedback
  - Optimization: Tweak performance, add features
  - ROI tracking: Document time savings, revenue impact
  - Renewal: Ongoing retainer or next phase
- **Exit Criteria:** Contract renewal or upsell

## PART 8: ADDITIONAL RESOURCES

### 8.1 Prospect Research Framework

When adding new prospects to database:

1. **Company website review:**
   - Look for: Pain points mentioned, tools used, team size, service offerings
   - Identify: Decision maker (usually owner, operations manager)

2. **Tools identification:**
   - Check: What booking/CRM platform do they use?
   - Check: LinkedIn to see team structure

3. **Personalization angle:**
   - Find: Specific, recent change or challenge (new location, team growth, etc.)
   - Find: Competitor or similar business (what are they doing?)

4. **Fit scoring:**
   - High Fit (0.8-1.0): Clear pain point + right tool stack + team size
   - Medium Fit (0.5-0.8): Some pain point visible + manageable tools
   - Low Fit (0-0.5): Generic operation + unclear pain points + complex tech stack

### 8.2 Assessment Call Script

**15-minute discovery call structure:**

```
[0-1 min] Opening
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time. Here's how I think about this: 
I want to understand your business, your biggest challenges, 
and whether there's a real opportunity for us to work together. 
Sound good?"

[1-3 min] Warm-up Question
"Tell me about [Company]. What does a typical day look like for you?"
→ Listen for: Pain points hidden in their daily process

[3-7 min] Problem Deep-Dive
"You mentioned [pain point]. Walk me through that. How much time does that take? 
Who on your team handles it? What's the impact if it doesn't get done?"
→ Listen for: Quantifiable impact (time, money, customer experience)

[7-10 min] Tools & Opportunity
"What tools do you currently use? [They mention FareHarbor / Vagaro / etc.]
How integrated is everything? Any data moving between systems manually?"
→ Listen for: Integration gaps, manual processes, data flow

[10-13 min] Fit Assessment
"If we could automate [the pain point they mentioned], what would that be worth to you?"
→ Listen for: Willingness to pay, budget signals

[13-15 min] Next Steps
"Here's what I'm thinking: You're dealing with [their biggest problem], and I know 
how to solve this with a custom AI system. I'd like to build a quick analysis showing 
exactly how this would work for your business. Does that interest you?"
→ Close: Demo/proposal timeline if yes, or graceful exit if no fit
```
