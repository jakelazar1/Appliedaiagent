## OBJECTION HANDLER QUICK GUIDE

**When prospects say these, respond with this:**

```
OBJECTION: "Not the right time for us."

RESPOND WITH:
"I totally understand. Usually I hear that because it's not a priority 
yet. Two things:

1. If you'd like, I can send you a quick analysis of what's possible 
   for [Company] — no obligation. Just so you have it if timing changes.

2. Can I ask: What would need to happen for this to become a priority? 
   [Listen] Then we can stay connected around that."

OUTCOME: Get them thinking about it + stay in pipeline

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OBJECTION: "This sounds like software we'd need to manage."

RESPOND WITH:
"I get it—nobody wants another software tool. That's actually why 
I do this differently.

I'm not selling you software. I'm embedding with you to build a 
custom system that handles [their pain point]. My job is to set it 
up, train your team, and stay involved making sure it works.

You don't manage it. It just works. And I stay accountable."

OUTCOME: Reframe from "platform" to "partnership"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OBJECTION: "We already tried automation and it didn't work."

RESPOND WITH:
"That's actually really common. Why didn't it work? [Listen]

Here's the difference with what I do: Most automation tools are 
one-size-fits-all. They don't fit your specific workflow.

What I do is different. I spend 2-3 weeks learning your exact process, 
then build something custom for you. That's why it actually works.

Can I just show you what that looks like for [Company]?"

OUTCOME: Distinguish from their failed experience + move forward

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OBJECTION: "How much does this cost?"

RESPOND WITH:
"Great question. It depends on scope + complexity. But typically:

- Custom build (2-3 weeks): $5,000-15,000
- Monthly optimization retainer: $2,000-7,000/month

For most [their vertical] owners, that's worth it because they reclaim 
$X/month in time + $Y/month in revenue impact.

But let's not talk price yet. First, let's make sure this is actually 
a fit for you. Can we schedule a quick assessment call?"

OUTCOME: Give ballpark without committing + stay focused on fit

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OBJECTION: "I need to think about it."

RESPOND WITH:
"Of course. That makes sense.

To help with your thinking, can I ask: What are the main questions 
you need answered? [Listen]

I can send you something that addresses that. Then, if it makes sense, 
we can talk next week."

OUTCOME: Reduce friction to next step + stay engaged

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OBJECTION: "I'll need to discuss this with my team/partner."

RESPOND WITH:
"Totally makes sense. You shouldn't decide alone.

Here's what I'd suggest: Can you bring [team/partner] on a quick call 
with me? 15 minutes. I'll walk you both through it, and then you can 
discuss together.

That way everyone's on the same page. Sound good?"

OUTCOME: Get more decision makers + accelerate process

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OBJECTION: "Your competitors offer the same thing cheaper."

RESPOND WITH:
"Could be. But here's what's different:

I don't just hand you software. I embed with your team, build something 
custom for your workflow, train everyone, and stay involved optimizing 
it.

Most competitors won't do that. They sell you a template and disappear.

That's why it's worth the difference. I'm betting my reputation on it 
working for you."

OUTCOME: Defend value + build trust

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OBJECTION: "We're happy with how we're running things now."

RESPOND WITH:
"That's great. But let me ask you this:

Are you happy with how things are, or are you just used to how things are?

[Pause]

Because most [their vertical] owners I talk with are frustrated with 
15+ hours/week spent on [their pain point]. They're just not sure 
there's an alternative.

Can I just show you what's possible? No commitment. Just so you know 
what's possible if things get overwhelming."

OUTCOME: Plant seeds of dissatisfaction + show possibility

```
