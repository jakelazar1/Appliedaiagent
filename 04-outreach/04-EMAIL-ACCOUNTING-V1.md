# Cold Email — Accounting & Bookkeeping (V1)

Vertical: Solo CPAs, small firms, independent bookkeepers (Sonoma/Napa)
Wedge: Untracked recurring revenue (one-time clients who should be recurring +
clients who quietly didn't return)
Voice: Jake — real builder, no jargon, no emojis. Matches AppliedAIAgent.ai
teammate positioning ("I work while you're closed / I ask before anything goes out").

Rules of use:
- Personalize the [brackets] per recipient. Do NOT send the template raw.
- Send to a VERIFIED direct owner email only. Never info@.
- Draft to Gmail first for review before sending.

## PRIMARY VERSION

Subject: the clients [Firm] should be billing monthly

Hi [FirstName] —

Quick one. Most practices your size have two quiet leaks: clients who only
come in once a year for tax prep but really should be on monthly bookkeeping,
and clients who just didn't come back this year — and nobody noticed until
it was too late to ask why.

There's usually no system flagging either one. It's not a discipline problem,
it's that you're heads-down doing the work, not watching the list.

I build an AI teammate that watches it for you. It keeps track of who's
one-time vs. recurring, flags the clients drifting toward not coming back,
and tees up the "you should really be on a monthly plan" conversation before
the moment passes. It checks with you before anything goes to a client — it
doesn't act on its own.

I'm Jake, I'm in Sonoma, and I build these one practice at a time and stay
responsible for them. I'd build yours around how [Firm] actually runs, not
some template.

Worth a 15-minute call to see if the math is there for you?

Jake, hoping to hear back from you

## Personalization hooks (pick one, work it into line 1 or the sign-off)

  contractors / wineries] — that's a client base where recurring advisory
  really adds up").
- Reference solo vs. firm ("running solo, you ARE the system, which is exactly
  why nothing's watching the drift").

  first").
