## ANIMATIONS & MICRO-INTERACTIONS

### Entrance Animations
- **Fade-in on scroll:** Opacity 0→1, 600ms, ease-out
- **Stagger effect:** Each card in a group delays 100-150ms
- **Slide-up (optional):** Combined with fade (translateY 20px → 0)

### Hover Animations
- **Buttons:** Scale 1.05, shadow increase, 0.3s ease
- **Cards:** Scale 1.02, shadow increase, 0.3s ease
- **Links:** Color change + underline appears, 0.2s ease
- **Icons:** Color shift or rotate slightly, 0.3s ease

### Loading & Interactive
- **Form submit:** Button shows spinner or "Sending..." text
- **Success:** Brief checkmark animation
- **Smooth transitions:** All color/size changes 0.3s or less

### Avoid
- ❌ Auto-playing videos
- ❌ Jarring animations (>1s)
- ❌ Multiple competing animations
- ❌ Motion that distracts from content

## COLOR USAGE & CONTRAST

### Text Colors (FIXED)
**Primary text:** #1A1A1A (soft black, not harsh)
**Secondary text:** #3D3D3D (soft dark gray)
**Tertiary text:** #8B8680 (warm taupe)
**ALL TEXT MUST BE READABLE** — contrast ratio 4.5:1 minimum (WCAG AA)

### CTA/Accent Colors
- **Primary CTA:** Warm orange #E8845C
- **Hover:** Darker orange #D97560
- **Links:** Warm orange #E8845C (not blue)

### Backgrounds
- **Light sections:** #FFFBF7 (light cream) or #FEFDFB (nearly white)
- **Gradient sections:** #FFFBF7 → #FAF1E8 (subtle warm shift)
- **Dark sections:** #0D1F1A (forest green) or #2D4538 (warm green)

### Shadows
- **Soft:** rgba(0,0,0,0.04) on subtle elements
- **Medium:** rgba(0,0,0,0.08) on cards
- **Pronounced:** rgba(0,0,0,0.12) on elevated elements
- **NEVER:** Pure black shadows

## SPACING & WHITESPACE

### Section Gaps
- **Between major sections:** 140-160px vertical
- **Within sections:** 80-120px internal padding
- **Between elements:** 40-60px

### Container Widths
- **Max content width:** 1200px
- **Horizontal padding (desktop):** 80px
- **Horizontal padding (tablet):** 40px
- **Horizontal padding (mobile):** 20px

### Card Padding
- **Internal padding:** 40-48px
- **Don't touch edges:** Always leave 20px+ margin from container edge
- **Visual breathing room:** More white space > more content

## RESPONSIVE DESIGN

### Mobile (375-599px)
- Single column layouts
- Stacked cards
- Full-width CTAs
- Touch-friendly (48px minimum hit area)
- Simpler animations (fade only, no complex sequences)

### Tablet (600-1023px)
- 2-column grids where appropriate
- Some content stacking
- Moderate spacing
- All animations active

### Desktop (1024px+)
- 3+ column grids
- Asymmetric layouts
- Maximum spacing
- All animations and effects active
