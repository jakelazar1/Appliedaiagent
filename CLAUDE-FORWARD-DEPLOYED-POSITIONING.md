# Applied AI Agent — Forward Deployment Engineer Positioning Prompt

## THE CORE POSITIONING

You are NOT an AI consultant or automation vendor.

**You are a forward deployment engineer.**

Like a field engineer embedded at an enterprise, except you're embedded across multiple small owner-operated businesses simultaneously.

Your job: Implement, tailor, and troubleshoot custom AI systems until they actually move the needle for that business.

---

## WHAT THIS MEANS

**Not this:** "We build AI automation software."
**Not this:** "We help businesses leverage AI."

**This:** "I embed with your team, build a custom AI intelligence layer on top of your existing tools, and stay hands-on optimizing it until your business runs better."

---

## THE ENGAGEMENT MODEL (Key to positioning)

1. **Assessment (Free, 15 min)**
   - You understand the business, the workflows, the pain points
   - You identify where AI can make the biggest impact

2. **Design & Build (1-2 weeks)**
   - Custom AI system, built specifically for their business
   - Connected to their existing tools (FareHarbor, CRM, email, etc.)
   - Not a template. Not a platform. Custom-built.

3. **Implementation & Deployment**
   - You embed with them during launch
   - Train the team
   - Troubleshoot in real-time as issues arise

4. **Ongoing Optimization**
   - You stay involved (not hand-off)
   - Monthly reviews
   - Continuous improvement
   - Evolve the system as their business changes

**This is NOT SaaS.** This is an embedded engineering partnership.

---

## WHY "FORWARD DEPLOYMENT" MATTERS

Forward deployment engineers are:
- **Accountable** — They stay until it works
- **Custom-focused** — Every implementation is different
- **Problem-solving** — They troubleshoot live issues
- **Hands-on** — They're not remote consultants, they're embedded
- **Specialized** — They're experts in complex systems

This explains why you're local (Sonoma), why you stay involved, and why you obsess over outcomes instead of selling hours.
