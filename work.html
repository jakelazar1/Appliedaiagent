What I build.

Six categories of automation. Every system is custom-built, connected to your existing tools, and running within 48 hours of the first call.

Scheduling

Your calendar, handled.

New requests acknowledged in minutes. Confirmation and reminder sequences go out automatically. No more back-and-forth over availability.

  • Booking confirmation sent immediately on request
  • Reminder sequence: 24 hrs out, 2 hrs out, post-appointment
  • Reschedule and cancellation handling without phone tag
  • Connects to Calendly, Google Calendar, Tock, FareHarbor
Lead Follow-Up

Your leads never go cold.

Every new inquiry gets an instant, personal response. A follow-up sequence runs until they book or opt out. No leads falling through the cracks.

  • Response under 5 minutes, any hour, any day
  • Qualification questions asked automatically
  • Warm leads escalated to you at the right moment
  • Connects to HubSpot, Gmail, any CRM with an API
Communications

The right message, at the right time.

Post-visit emails, review requests, seasonal outreach. Triggered by what your customers actually do — not a blast calendar you have to remember.

  • Post-visit thank-you and follow-up sequence
  • Review request timed for peak response rate
  • Re-engagement campaigns triggered by inactivity
  • Connects to Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Twilio, Google Business
Retention

Keep the customers you have.

An agent that watches engagement signals, identifies at-risk customers before they disappear, and triggers the right outreach before the relationship goes cold.

  • Engagement monitoring across email, bookings, and purchases
  • Churn signal detection before cancellation
  • Personalized re-engagement sequence triggered automatically
  • High-value accounts flagged for personal follow-up
Operations Dashboard

See your business in real time.

A custom dashboard showing everything happening in your operation — automations running, leads in progress, revenue trends, customer activity. Built in React for your specific operation.

  • Live automation activity feed

  • Custom reports for your specific operation
