# Applied AI Agent — Design Brief for Framer

## THE CONCEPT

Design a website for a forward deployment AI engineer based in Sonoma, CA. This is NOT a SaaS platform, NOT a consultant, but an engineer who embeds directly with small businesses to build, implement, and optimize custom AI systems.

---

## CORE MESSAGE

**Headline:** "I build custom AI systems. Then I stay embedded."

**Subheading:** "An AI engineer embedded in your business. I implement custom systems, train your team, and stay hands-on optimizing until your operation actually runs better."

---

## VISUAL DIRECTION

- **Aesthetic:** Light, clean, lovely to look at (not dark/corporate)
- **Color palette:** Light backgrounds (cream/white), one warm accent color (could be gold, rust, warm gray, or soft blue)
- **Typography:** Modern, elegant, breathing room (generous spacing)
- **Tone:** Professional but human, technical but approachable
- **Motion:** Smooth animations where they make sense (not gimmicky)
- **Vibe:** Feels like a real person's website, not a templated agency site

---

## STRUCTURE (What the site should show)

### 1. HERO
- Bold headline: "I build custom AI systems. Then I stay embedded."
- 1-2 sentence subheading explaining what that means
- CTA: "Schedule a free assessment"
- Optional: A visual (diagram, code snippet, or illustration showing "embedded" concept)

### 2. WHAT I BUILD (Brief section)
- 4 example verticals: Tour Operators / Wineries / Home Services / Real Estate
- Each with a 1-2 sentence description of the AI system
- Visual: Could be icons, cards, or interactive tabs
- NO LONG COPY — keep it punchy

### 3. HOW IT WORKS (4-step process)
- Step 1: Assessment (understand the business)
- Step 2: Design & Build (custom system)
- Step 3: Deploy & Embed (launch with your team)
- Step 4: Ongoing Optimization (stay hands-on)
- Each step: 1 sentence max + optional visual (icon, diagram, or animation)

### 4. WHY (Brief credibility section)
- 2-3 sentences about who you are
- Example: "I spent 10 years in wine operations before becoming an AI engineer. I know how small businesses work. I build systems tailored to your workflows, then stay embedded optimizing until they actually move the needle."
- NO LONG PARAGRAPHS

### 5. CTA / CONTACT
- "Ready to embed an AI system in your business?"
- CTA: "Schedule a free assessment"
- Email: jake@appliedaiagent.ai

---

## DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

- Keep it simple (no fluff, no unnecessary sections)
- Mobile responsive
- Fast loading (no heavy animations that slow it down)
- Professional but warm (not cold/corporate)
- Show, don't tell (use visuals + short copy, not paragraphs)

---

## TONE OF COPY

- Direct, clear, specific
- Technical but accessible
- Conversational (like a real person)
- NO corporate buzzwords ("leverage," "unlock," "transform")
- NO emojis

---

## OPTIONAL EXTRAS (If you want to show off)

- Smooth fade-in animations on scroll
- Micro-interactions on buttons/CTAs
- A simple diagram showing the "embedding" concept
- Subtle background elements (not distracting)
- Dark mode toggle (if you want, not required)

---

## THE ASK

Show me what a clean, simple, beautiful website for a forward deployment AI engineer could look like. Make it feel approachable, not corporate. Make it look lovely to look at.

---

## REFERENCE INSPIRATION (Optional)

If you want reference points:
- Stripe.com (light, elegant)
- Notion.so (warm, human, minimal)
- Framer.com (modern, clean, motion-forward)
- Cal.com (simple, airy)

But don't copy them — just capture that vibe (light on the eyes, lovely, clean).

---

## SUCCESS

When you're done, the site should:
- [ ] Communicate what I do in 3 seconds (I embed with businesses to build/optimize AI systems)
- [ ] Feel light and approachable (not dark/corporate)
- [ ] Look beautiful and intentional (not generic)
- [ ] Be mostly visual/motion with minimal copy
- [ ] Have clear CTAs
- [ ] Load fast and work on mobile
